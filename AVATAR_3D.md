# 3D avatar (v3)

`sd_base_v3` on the `sd_rig_v3` skeleton is a stylised game character, rebuilt from the
ground up. It replaces `sd_base_v2`, which was a reskinned mannequin.

What's in it:

- A modelled face and hands.
- Strand-shaded hair with variants that fit under hats.
- Real beards.
- Clothes with thickness and trim, on a body that hides the skin they cover.
- A socket-based attachment system for rigid wearables.
- Blendshapes for build, face options and expressions.
- PBR studio rendering.

The customisation UI, inventory, saving, equipped data and server validation are
unchanged. Saved looks keep working: `sd_base_v1` and `sd_base_v2` render on v3.

## 1. Diagnosis (v2)

Root causes, not symptoms:

| Symptom | Root cause | v3 fix |
|---|---|---|
| Mannequin look: small head, long neck, flat face, painted-on eyes, mouth as a line | One SDF with mannequin proportions; no eyelids, lashes, lips or nose structure; head 1.0× | Re-proportioned body (~6.3 heads tall, head ~1.15× realistic, short strong neck). Sculpted cranium, cheekbones, jaw and nose. Modelled lips with a mouth cavity and teeth. Eyeballs with iris, pupil, upper and lower lids, lashes. Brows are geometry. |
| Mitten hands, block feet | Two-segment fingers, no thumb opposition; feet without toes or arch | Five three-joint fingers in a relaxed curl. Feet with toes and an arch, weighted to `ToeBase`. |
| Plastic hair, no beards | One shell per style, MeshStandard, no strand direction | Hair modelled per style with strand normal detail and a strand-direction (anisotropy) map. Beards and goatees are volumes that taper into a soft edge shadow; stubble is a translucent shell. |
| Hats float or sink and cover the eyes; cap sat high | Hats placed on the `Head` bone at fixed offsets, unaware of the hairstyle. Hair had only two crude "under hat" variants. | Shared fit volumes (`fit.mjs`): every hat's inner surface is built a set distance off the scalp, and every hairstyle ships a pressed variant per hat mode. Hats hang from a `HeadTop` socket, with per-item attachment specs that resolve per hairstyle, face, build and other worn items. |
| Brown underwear showing through shorts; skin through cloth when animating | Layers were 4–7 mm apart, which is less than mesh decimation error. Everything stayed drawn. | The body is split into 15 regions. A garment declares the regions it covers (`hides`), measured by `fitcheck.mjs`, and those stop drawing. The base layer goes when hips are covered. Layer offsets keep trim ≥ 3.5 mm above the cloth beneath it. |
| Waistband streaking through tees and hoodies | Garment materials used a slope-scaled `polygonOffsetFactor`. On edge-on faces (a waistband's top seen past a tee) it pulled the inner layer millimetres forward. | Constant depth bias only. The camera's depth range is also fitted to the viewing distance (`fitDepthRange`). |
| Pixel cracks along body seams | Each region mesh was quantised on its own grid, so shared border vertices rounded differently | One quantisation grid per file (`quantizationVolume: "scene"`) |
| Body type stretched garments and accessories | Build was done by scaling bones | Build is a `buildSlim` / `buildBroad` blendshape pair on the body, head, beard and every skinned garment. Attachments read the build (`perBuild`). |
| Flat lighting, no depth | MeshStandard, no environment, two lights, no shadows | Physical materials (fabric sheen, clearcoat eyes and lenses, anisotropic hair, skin sheen). Baked normal maps and AO. ACES. PMREM studio environment. Key, fill and two rims. Soft shadow map on the live stage. Contact shadow. |
| 3 MB base download, everything at once | 4 heads and 27 hair meshes in one file, float32 attributes | Hair and facial hair are separate files, loaded per look. Files are quantised (`KHR_mesh_quantization`), morph targets are sparse, normal maps are JPEG. Base file: see §6. |

## 2. Asset pipeline

### 2.1 Procedural generator (the bundled set)

All bundled assets are generated by `artifacts/mobile/scripts/avatar/`. The pipeline:

- **Modelling.** Each part is a signed-distance field.
- **Meshing.** Surface nets turn the field into a mesh.
- **Simplifying.** meshoptimizer reduces it with an absolute error bound (sub-millimetre for faces), so small features survive.
- **Detail.** Normals come from the field gradient. AO is baked into vertex colours. The field, plus each template's fabric or strand detail, is baked into a tangent-space normal map. That map is filtered to the texel size and packed into one atlas per file with smooth gutters.
- **Export.** GLB, quantised, sparse morph targets.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `rig.mjs` | `sd_rig_v3`: Mixamo bone names, rest positions, sockets, layer offsets, body regions, option ids |
| `anatomy.mjs`, `character.mjs` | Body and head fields, build controls, region map, face proportions |
| `face.mjs`, `expressions.mjs` | Eyes, lids, lashes, brows and teeth; ARKit blink, smile and jawOpen as deformations |
| `hair.mjs`, `fit.mjs` | Hairstyles, beards, and the headwear fit volumes shared by hats and hair |
| `templates.mjs`, `attachments.mjs` | 21 garment templates; attachment specs (crown seat per hairstyle, chain over tops, watch past cuffs) |
| `bake.mjs`, `jpeg.mjs`, `glb.mjs` | Atlas and normal-map baking, JPEG encoder, GLB reader and writer |
| `generate.mjs` | Builds every asset: `node scripts/avatar/generate.mjs [--only k1,k2] [--jobs 2] [--preview]` |
| `validate.mjs`, `fitcheck.mjs`, `hatfit.mjs`, `budget.mjs`, `registry.mjs` | Checks (§9) and the bundle registry |

Outputs in `assets/avatar/`:

- `sd_base_v3.glb`: body regions, base layer, four heads, eyes, lids, lashes, brows, teeth, sockets, and the `idle` and `cheer` clips.
- `hair_<style>.glb`: the free hairstyle, plus one pressed variant per hat mode.
- `beard_<style>.glb`: one beard per face shape, plus its edge shadow.
- `<template>.glb`: one per garment.

Compression is glTF-Transform's `quantize` and `sparse`. glTF-Transform is an optional dev dependency, loaded dynamically: without it the generator writes unquantised files. Install it once with `pnpm add -D --filter @workspace/mobile @gltf-transform/core@4.5.1 @gltf-transform/functions@4.5.1 @gltf-transform/extensions@4.5.1`.

Draco and Meshopt were not used: their decoders need WASM and Workers, which expo-gl and Hermes don't provide. Quantisation, sparse accessors and JPEG all decode in plain GLTFLoader.

### 2.2 Blender pipeline (hand-authored or remote assets)

1. **Start from the rig.** Import `assets/avatar/sd_base_v3.glb`.
   - Units are metres, +Y up (glTF), and the character faces +Z.
   - Don't rename, move or rotate bones. Every bone's rest rotation is identity.
   - A Mixamo armature also works: `mixamorig:` / `mixamorig` prefixes are stripped at load.
2. **Model on the base**, in its rest pose.
   - Use the layer offsets in §2.4.
   - Give cloth real thickness (Solidify 1.5–3 mm) or model one surface and leave it double-sided.
   - Add hems, cuffs, collars and seams as geometry or normal detail.
3. **Skin it.**
   - Data-transfer the weights from the body (nearest face interpolated), then clean them up.
   - At most 4 influences per vertex, normalised.
   - Rigid items (hats, glasses, jewellery) are parented to the socket empty (`Socket_HeadTop`, `Socket_Face`, `Socket_Neck`, `Socket_Wrist_L/R`), not skinned.
4. **Add shape keys.**
   - Skinned garments: `buildSlim` and `buildBroad`. Transfer them from the body with a Surface Deform modifier bound at Basis: apply as shape key with the body at each key.
   - Facial items that sit on the face (masks, beards): `noseStraight`, `noseBroad`, `lipsFull`, `mouthWide`, `jawOpen`, `mouthSmileLeft`, `mouthSmileRight`.
   - Keys must be relative to Basis, with exact names.
5. **Name things.**
   - Materials follow the tint contract:
     - `primary`, `secondary`, `primary_dark`, `secondary_dark` take the item's colours;
     - `metal`, `lens`, `laces`, `sole` keep their own look.
   - Mesh names are free for garments.
   - Base and hair files use the fixed names in §2.3.
6. **Textures.**
   - Base colour stays white or flat; colour comes from the tint.
   - Normal map: OpenGL convention (glTF), ≤ 1024 px; ≤ 512 for small accessories.
   - JPEG for normal maps; PNG only where alpha is needed.
   - Optional `KHR_materials_sheen` for fabric, `KHR_materials_clearcoat` for gloss, `KHR_materials_anisotropy` for hair.
7. **Metadata.** Put it in the scene's custom properties, with "Include → Custom Properties" ticked on export:
   ```json
   {"sdAvatar": {"rig": "sd_rig_v3", "version": 3, "kind": "garment", "template": "top_mytee",
     "slot": "top", "hairMode": null, "excludes": [], "hides": ["chest", "abdomen"], "layer": 3,
     "attach": {"socket": "HeadTop", "position": [0, 0, 0], "rotation": [0, 0, 0], "scale": 1,
                "perHair": {"afro": {"position": [0, 0.02, 0]}}}}}
   ```
8. **Export.**
   - glTF Binary, +Y up.
   - Apply modifiers. Skinning on, shape keys on (shape-key normals optional), tangents off.
   - Limit to 4 influences and normalise.
   - No animation unless the file is a clip.
9. **Compress and check.**
   ```bash
   npx @gltf-transform/cli quantize in.glb out.glb && npx @gltf-transform/cli sparse out.glb out.glb
   cd artifacts/mobile
   node scripts/avatar/validate.mjs path/to/out.glb       # rig, metadata, weights, budgets
   node scripts/avatar/fitcheck.mjs --hides <id>           # measure what it covers (copy into metadata)
   node scripts/avatar/fitcheck.mjs <id>                   # nothing pokes through; declared hides really covered
   node scripts/avatar/hatfit.mjs <id>                     # headwear: every face × hairstyle
   node scripts/avatar/budget.mjs <id> top_tee bottom_shorts shoes_sneaker --hair afro   # whole avatar ≤ 30k
   ```
10. **Ship.**
    - Bundled: drop the file in `assets/avatar/` and run `node scripts/avatar/registry.mjs`.
    - Remote: upload it, then point a catalog document's `assetUrl` at it (§4). No app release is needed.

### 2.3 Naming conventions

| Thing | Name |
|---|---|
| Bones | Mixamo: `Hips`, `Spine`, `Spine1`, `Spine2`, `Neck`, `Head`, `Left/RightShoulder`, `Arm`, `ForeArm`, `Hand`, `UpLeg`, `Leg`, `Foot`, `ToeBase` |
| Sockets | `Socket_HeadTop` (crown), `Socket_Face` (nose bridge), `Socket_Neck` (base of neck, front), `Socket_Wrist_L/R` |
| Base meshes | `Body_<region>` × 15, `Underwear`, `Head_<oval\|round\|square\|heart>`, `Eyes`, `Iris`, `Pupils`, `Teeth`, `Lids_<round\|almond\|sleepy>`, `Lashes_<…>`, `Brows_<soft\|arched\|bold>` |
| Hair meshes | `Hair`, `Hair_<capped\|covered\|helmet\|banded>`, `HairTie[_<mode>]` (no tie variant for a mode means the hat covers it) |
| Facial hair | `Beard_<face>`, `BeardShadow_<face>` |
| Body regions | `neck shoulders chest abdomen waist hips upperArms elbows forearms hands thighs knees shins ankles feet` |
| Blendshapes | `buildSlim`, `buildBroad`, `noseStraight`, `noseBroad`, `lipsFull`, `mouthWide`, `eyeBlinkLeft`, `eyeBlinkRight`, `mouthSmileLeft`, `mouthSmileRight`, `jawOpen` (ARKit names) |
| Garment files and templates | `<slot>_<name>`: `top_tee`, `hat_cap`, `acc_watch` |

### 2.4 Garment checklist

- [ ] Built on `sd_base_v3` in its rest pose. Feet are on the ground plane (y = 0).
- [ ] Surface off the skin at the layer offset:
  - base layer 4 mm, bottoms 6 mm, shoes 12 mm, tops 15 mm (21 mm below 1.06 m, over the waistband), outerwear 30 mm;
  - every trim (band, cuff, print, pocket, zip) ≥ 3.5 mm above the cloth under it.
- [ ] Thickness at hems, cuffs and collars, and no open edges you can see into.
- [ ] Skinned:
  - ≤ 4 weights per vertex, normalised;
  - weights transferred from the body, so it bends with the body;
  - `buildSlim` / `buildBroad` keys.
- [ ] Rigid: parented to the right socket, with an `attach` spec.
  - `perHair` / `perFace` / `perBuild` where fit differs.
  - `with` for items it must clear or hide under.
- [ ] Materials follow the tint contract. Normal map ≤ 1024 px (≤ 512 for small accessories).
- [ ] ≤ 5,000 triangles (slot targets: top and outerwear 4k, bottom 3.6k, shoes 3.2k, hat and accessory 2.8k, eyewear 1.4k). File ≤ 600 KB.
- [ ] `hides` measured with `fitcheck.mjs --hides`. Declare only regions at 100 % covered.
- [ ] `fitcheck.mjs` passes: no skin over cloth in the rest pose.
- [ ] Headwear:
  - `hairMode` set (`capped`, `covered`, `helmet`, `banded`, or none for items that sit on the hair, like crowns);
  - inner surface on that mode's fit surface;
  - `hatfit.mjs` passes for every face × hairstyle.
- [ ] Whole preset outfits stay ≤ 30k triangles (`budget.mjs`).
- [ ] Before/after renders of the presets that use it: no clipping, no z-fighting, no floating.

## 3. Rendering

All of this is in `lib/avatar3d/avatarRig.ts` and shared by the live stage and the portrait studio.

- **Materials:** physical (glTF PBR + extensions).
  - **Fabric:** sheen per fabric preset, tinted to the cloth colour, so dark cloth isn't washed out.
  - **Eyes and lenses:** clearcoat.
  - **Skin:** sheen and a faint warm emissive in place of subsurface scattering.
  - **Hair and beard:** sheen, plus anisotropy along the strand map. The sheen follows the tinted colour, which stops dark hair turning silver at grazing angles.
- **Detail:** baked normal maps on everything. Ambient occlusion in vertex colours (armpits, between fingers, under the collar).
- **Lighting:**
  - a hemisphere bounce;
  - a warm key, which casts a 1024 soft PCF shadow on the live stage only;
  - a cool fill;
  - an amber rim and a cool rim.
- **Environment:** a PMREM `RoomEnvironment` at 0.42, on WebGL2 (lights only elsewhere).
- **Tone and colour:** ACES Filmic with sRGB output (R3F defaults).
- **Contact shadow:** a textured quad under the feet.
- **Depth:**
  - layers are separated geometrically;
  - a constant per-layer depth bias breaks ties;
  - the near plane is fitted to the viewing distance (`fitDepthRange`), so 16-bit depth buffers still resolve millimetre layers.
- **Animation:**
  - **Idle:** 8 s of breathing, weight shift, soft elbows and head drift. Each avatar starts at a random phase.
  - **Cheer:** a reaction with a grin and an open jaw.
  - **Face:** random blinks, sometimes a double blink, and a resting hint of a smile.

## 4. Firestore data model

```ts
// users/{uid}
avatar: {
  height: 0..1, bodyType: 0..1,                        // height = uniform scale ±7 %, bodyType = build blendshapes
  skinTone, hairColor, eyeColor,                        // palette hex values
  hairStyle, faceShape, eyeShape, eyebrows,             // option ids (rules)
  facialHair, noseShape, mouthShape,                    // new in v3, optional, default none/button/neutral
  base: "sd_base_v3",                                   // v1 and v2 still validate and render on v3
  faceStyle?                                            // legacy, ignored
}
equipped: { hat?, eyewear?, top?, outerwear?, bottom?, shoes?, accessory? }   // catalog ids

// catalog/{itemId}
{
  id, name, description, slot, price, rarity, available, releaseAt, thumbnailUrl,
  color, secondaryColor,              // tint the template's primary / secondary materials
  model?: "top_hoodie",               // bundled template (one template serves any number of items)
  assetUrl?: "https://…/item.glb",    // remote GLB on sd_rig_v3; overrides model; no app release
  assetVersion?: 3,                   // bump when the file at assetUrl changes (cache key)
  excludes?: Slot[],                  // slots it can't be worn with (template default if omitted)
  hairMode?: "capped"|"covered"|"helmet"|"banded",  // headwear only
  hides?: BodyRegion[],               // regions it fully covers (asset metadata if omitted)
  layer?: number,                     // inner → outer draw order (slot default: shoes 1 … outerwear 4, rigid 5)
  attach?: AttachSpec                 // merged over the asset's own spec — nudge one item without touching the file
}
```

A new item is a catalog write. A new colourway needs only `color` / `secondaryColor`. A new mesh needs `assetUrl`, plus `hides`, `layer`, `hairMode` and `attach` when the file's metadata doesn't already carry them.

Rules live in `lib/avatar3d/avatarRules.ts`, which is copied byte-for-byte to `functions/src/lib/avatarRules.ts`; a test enforces that. The server validates appearance, ownership, slots and exclusions (`saveAppearance`, `equipItem`). Firestore rules are unchanged, because clients still can't write `users/{uid}`.

## 5. Runtime code

| Concern | Where |
|---|---|
| Loading and caching | `loader.ts`: one GLTFLoader cache. Remote files go through `expo-asset`'s download cache on native, keyed by URL + `?v=assetVersion`, so they survive restarts. `rigNames.ts` strips Mixamo prefixes from bones and clips. |
| Which files a look needs | `useAvatarAssets.ts`: base, one hair file, one facial-hair file and each garment, loaded in parallel and lazily. `outfit.ts` turns equipped items + the catalog into pieces (template or URL, tint, hides, layer, attach, version) and a look key. |
| Garment attach | `AvatarRig.setOutfit`. Skinned meshes rebind to the character's bones by name. Rigid parts hang from sockets, placed by `attach.ts` `resolveAttach(spec, { hairStyle, faceShape, build, worn })`: the asset's spec merged with the catalog override. Positions and rotations add; scales multiply. |
| Region hiding | `updateVisibility`. The union of worn items' `hides` stops drawing those `Body_*` meshes. The base layer shows until something covers the hips. Each hairstyle shows its variant for the strongest worn hair mode. |
| Tinting | Per-instance material copies. `tint()` recolours by material name, with sheen following the colour. Garments fill `primary`, `secondary` and the `_dark` variants from the item. |
| Blendshapes | `setAppearance`: build, nose and mouth on every morphing mesh. `setExpression(name, weight)` overrides the ARKit channels; blinks and smiles run in `update(dt)`. |
| Idle / cheer | `playIdle()`, `cheer()` on the baked clips. |

| Tops (tee, long sleeve, jersey, hoodie) | 3,000 | 298–536 KB | 5k / 600 KB |
| Outerwear (bomber, varsity, puffer) | 3,200 | 394–416 KB | 5k / 600 KB |
| Bottoms (shorts, pants, joggers) | 2,800 | 308–445 KB | 5k / 600 KB |
| Shoes (sneaker, high-top) | 2,400 | 176–202 KB | 5k / 600 KB |
| Hats (cap, beanie, bucket, helmet; crown) | 2,175–2,199; 2,798 | 101–142 KB | 5k / 600 KB |
| Eyewear, chain, watch, headphones | 1,098–2,280 | 48–107 KB | 5k / 600 KB |

A look loads the base, one hair file, an optional beard and its garments. That's about 3–5 MB, and each file is cached after its first load. The bundled set is 15 MB on disk (v2: 6 MB), most of it the eight hair files, each carrying its under-hat variants.

Whole-avatar budget (`budget.mjs`): the eight presets draw 23.5k–29.8k triangles (limit 30k). That counts skin hidden under clothes as not drawn, one head and lid variant, the hair variant for the worn hat, and one beard.

Textures:

- 1024² for the body, the heads, hair, tops, outerwear and bottoms;
- 512² for shoes, hats, accessories and beards;
- 256² for eyewear.

Normal maps are JPEG. Hair anisotropy maps are at half resolution.

## 9. Checks and tests run

All run on the shipped files in `assets/avatar/`:

- **`validate.mjs` — 33/33 pass.** It checks the rig and metadata; that every skin joint is a rig bone with the right parent and rest offset; sockets; normalised weights; and the budgets in §6.
- **`fitcheck.mjs` — 21/21 garments pass.** No skin shows through cloth in the rest pose (the varsity is worst, with 6 vertices = 0.22 %). Every declared `hides` region is 100 % covered: no vertex uncovered, and ≥ 98.5 % at least 15 mm from uncovered skin.
- **`hatfit.mjs` — 189/189 pass.** That is every hat and the headphones × four face shapes × nine hairstyles. It checks:
  - **Hair through the item:** no hair pokes through within the covered region (limit 1 %).
  - **Inner gap:** ≤ 6.5 mm; helmet ≤ 13 mm because of the padding; headband ≤ 7.5 mm.
  - **Sink:** nothing sinks into the head by more than 4 mm. Ears are excluded, since they sit inside cups and guards by design.
  - **Eyes and brims:** nothing over the eyes, and brims clear the brows.
  - **Crown seat:** within 5.5 mm per azimuth, measured on the ring's lowest edge.
- **`budget.mjs` — every preset × {default, long hair + beard + square face, afro + goatee} is ≤ 30k.** The range is 23.5k–29.8k.
- **`cd functions && npm test` — 59/59 pass.** 21 of them are avatar tests:
  - the rules are byte-identical in the app and functions;
  - the GLB metadata matches `TEMPLATE_INFO`;
  - the base has every region, face variant and blendshape;
  - every hair mode and beard variant is present;
  - catalog and demo data agree;
  - editor option lists are complete;
  - appearance validation and sanitising, including v1 and v2 looks;
  - outfit resolution and exclusions;
  - catalog overrides (`hides`, `layer`, `hairMode`, `attach`) and `perBuild`;
  - Mixamo names;
  - validator mutations;
  - fit, hides and preset budgets;
  - the callables.
- **`tsc --noEmit`** is clean for the app and for functions.
- **Visual QA:** the sheets in `docs/avatar/` (§8), plus close-ups of hems, beards, shades on each face shape, and ponytail ties under hats.

### Known limits

- **Native not run here.** No simulator was available, so everything above is web (SwiftShader). Phase 1 is the device pass.
- **Long hair, braids and the pressed afro are stylised volumes, not cards.** Close up they read as sculpted shapes, and the afro under a brim flattens into a band.
- **The crown is rigid.** On uneven hair (fade, afro, braids) it settles up to 5 mm into the fullest parts and floats as much over the thinnest.
- **glTF-Transform is an optional dev dependency.** It is not in `package.json`, because adding it rewrote much of the lockfile. Install it to regenerate compressed files (§2.1).
- **Purchases in demo mode** still call the real callable. Try-on works.
